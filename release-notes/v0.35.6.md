# Changelog

### Improvements

- Treat manually entered historical energy figures as complete aggregate totals from the selected start date through the date they are saved.
- Resume meter-based calculations after the saved historical cutoff, without prorating aggregate totals across partial periods.
- Migrate existing historical aggregate entries safely by recording their cutoff during upgrade.
